Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

“Contos do Escuro Publisher” (the “App”) is a personal content-automation tool used by its owner to publish their own short videos to their own social media accounts, including TikTok. This policy explains what data the App handles.

What we access

When you connect a TikTok account, TikTok provides the App with an access token and basic account information (such as your open ID and display name) strictly so the App can upload videos to that same account on your behalf. The App only ever acts on the account of the person who authorised it.

What we store

The App stores only the OAuth access and refresh tokens needed to keep posting, on a private server controlled by the owner. It does not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties, and it does not collect data about anyone other than the authorising account owner.

What we do NOT do

We do not build advertising profiles, we do not access your private messages, and we do not read other users’ personal data.

Data deletion

You can revoke the App’s access at any time from your TikTok account settings (Security & permissions → Manage app permissions). Revoking access invalidates the stored tokens. To request deletion of any stored tokens, contact us below.
